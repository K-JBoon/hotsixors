## Overview

Leoric is an extremely strong laner, offering high sustain, strong waveclear from level 1 and good scaling into the late-game
with various unique playstyles. That said, he is very vulnerable to high mobility, high range and burst. He is best picked in
the last phase of the draft when the enemy team has shown their damage structure.
