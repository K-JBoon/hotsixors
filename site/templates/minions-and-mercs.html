Minions & Mercs

Lane minion XP uses the experience-globe system: the amount comes from the ExperienceAcquiredMinions effect (granted instantly when an enemy Hero lands the last hit, otherwise dropped as a globe pickup) and rises every minute. Mercenaries and bosses grant their unit's kill XP directly.


  • effectdata.xml — ExperienceAcquiredMinions base globe XP (Tomb override)
  • gamelib.galaxy — ExperienceGlobeSpawn / GrantXP / Scaling who drops globes, how XP is awarded, +2 per minute scaling
